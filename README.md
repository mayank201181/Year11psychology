# Year 11 IGCSE Psychology (`0266`) — Vanishree's Interactive Study Lab

Built for **Vanishree** (Year 11, Tanglin Trust School, Singapore) covering the full **Cambridge IGCSE Psychology (`0266`, 2027–2029)** specification across **Paper 1** and **Paper 2** with **zero login required**.

## Features
- **Zero-Login Automatic Progress Persistence (`y11psy.*`)**: Opens immediately as `"Vanishree"` on iPad, mobile, or desktop — saving stars, streaks, Daily 5, in-progress paper states, spaced repetition (`[1, 3, 7, 16, 35]` days), skill mastery, and watched episodes to `localStorage`.
- **Persistent 7-Tab Bottom Navigation Dock**: Always visible across mobile, iPad, and desktop (`Home · Topics · Watch · Sitcom · Daily 5 · Review · Progress`) with `pb-28` clearance.
- **7 Full CIE `0266` Topics (35 AoPS Discovery Lesson Sections + 18 Compulsory Named Studies)**:
  1. `memory-and-forgetting` — Multi-Store Model (Atkinson & Shiffrin; Murdock 1962), Working Memory (Baddeley & Hitch) & Levels of Processing (Craik & Tulving 1975), Reconstructive Schemas (Bartlett), Forgetting, Brain & Amnesia (Milner et al. 1968 on H.M.)
  2. `sleep-and-dreams` — NREM/REM Stages & Ultradian EEG Cycles, Circadian Rhythms (SCN & Melatonin), Restoration vs Evolutionary Theories, Insomnia & Sleep Hygiene, Freud vs Activation-Synthesis (Hobson & McCarley; Iorio et al.)
  3. `prosocial-behaviour` — Bystander Intervention (Latané & Darley 1968), Cost-Reward Model (Piliavin 1969; Levine et al.), Kin Selection & Genetics (Rushton et al.) vs Empathy-Altruism & Social Learning, Media & Parental Influences (Prot et al.)
  4. `visual-perception` — Sensation vs Perception, Depth Cues & Constancy, Gestalt Laws & Illusions, Gibson Direct Theory (Gibson & Walk 1960) vs Gregory Constructivist Theory (Haber & Levin 2001), Perceptual Set & Culture (Bremner et al. 2016)
  5. `motivation-and-needs` — Intrinsic/Extrinsic & Token Economies (Boniecki & Moore 2003), Maslow's Hierarchy, Self-Determination Theory (Calvo et al. 2010), Drive-Reduction, Incentive & Nostalgia (Merchant et al. 2013), SMART Goals
  6. `language-development` — Language Acquisition Stages & Overregularisation, Chomsky Nativist LAD (Senghas 1995), Behaviourist Learning (Goldstein, King & West 2003), Piaget Schemas (Hewitt 2022) & Bruner LASS, Animal vs Human Communication
  7. `research-methods` — Paper 1 Experimental Methods + Paper 2 Non-Experimental Methods, Ethics (DRIP), Reliability & Validity (GRAVE), Descriptive Stats & Graphs
- **CIE `0266` Exam Marks-Saver Spotlight & Printable Vault (`/formulas`)**: Side-by-side `Never Write This ❌ → Write This Instead ✅` mark-scheme comparisons, mnemonics, and key studies.
- **Interactive Explorables, 3-Tier Procedural Drills, 60s Sprint & 4 Full CIE `0266` Mock Papers** (`exam-p1a`, `exam-p1b`, `exam-p2a`, `exam-p2b`).
- **7 Narrated Animated Sitcom Episodes (`Mind Games: CIE 0266 Psychology`)**: Local neural TTS + canvas animation in `public/videos/`, each featuring Neuro-Board SVGs, Examiner Chen's Mark-Scheme Trap beat, and 3 pause challenges.
